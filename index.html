Everything you need to know

Sankalp’26 is organised by the IEEE Student Branch, AJIET, in association with the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering, AJIET, with support from IEEE Mangalore Subsection. Submit your idea by 8 November 2026. Selected teams will be announced on 20 November.

Who can participate

Open to all B.E. / B.Tech students from any college in India.

Team size

Every team must have exactly 3 registered members. Enter all three members’ names, USNs and email addresses in the idea form.

Selection and payment

All submitted ideas will be evaluated. Selected teams will be announced on 20 November and invited to complete final registration and payment through the official registration form. Payment deadline will be announced with the results.

  • At least one IEEE member: ₹500 per team
  • No IEEE members: ₹700 per team
  • Non-refundable once selected and registration is completed

Problem domains

AI and machine learning, healthcare, smart cities and sustainability, education and accessibility, IoT and embedded systems, or open innovation.

The final theme and any track restrictions will be announced by the committee.

Participant information

Rules & facilities

Please read these guidelines before submitting. Updates to rules and event arrangements will be shared through official channels.

Event rules

  1. Each team must have exactly three registered members.
  2. Only selected teams that complete registration and payment may participate.
  3. Teams must work on the idea submitted. Major changes require prior organiser approval.
  4. Projects must be developed or substantially improved during the 24-hour hackathon.
  5. Open-source libraries, frameworks, development tools, and AI tools are permitted. Acknowledge external code, datasets, APIs, and other resources.
  6. Plagiarism and presenting copied projects as original work are prohibited.
  7. Respect intellectual property rights, applicable laws, and the event code of conduct.
  8. Submit the final project, source code or repository link, and presentation before the announced deadline.
  9. Judges’ decisions are final, subject to the published event rules.
  10. Follow organiser instructions and maintain discipline throughout the event.

Evaluation guidelines

  • Innovation and originality: uniqueness of the idea and creativity of the proposed solution.
  • Technical implementation: technical complexity, implementation quality, and effective use of technology.
  • Problem relevance and impact: significance of the problem and potential real-world impact.
  • Functionality and prototype: working demonstration, usability, and completeness.
  • Presentation and teamwork: clarity, demonstration, and explanation of the solution.

Participant facilities

Availability will be confirmed by the organisers before the event.

  • Dedicated workspace for registered teams
  • Internet and Wi-Fi
  • Power and charging facilities
  • Mentorship and technical assistance
  • Food, refreshments, and drinking water
  • First-aid and emergency assistance

What to bring

  • Personal laptop and charger
  • Valid identification
  • Specialized hardware or equipment needed for your project

Check official announcements for facility confirmations and event updates.
