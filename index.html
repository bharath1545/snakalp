Everything you need to know

Sankalp’26 is organised by the IEEE Student Branch and the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering at AJIET, with support from IEEE Mangalore Subsection. Submit your idea by 25 October at 11:59 PM. The top 20 teams will be announced on 30 October and notified.

Who can participate

Open to all B.E. / B.Tech students from any college in India.

Team size

Maximum 4 members per team. Every member’s name, USN and email is needed when you submit your idea.

Selection and payment

The top 20 teams will be announced on 30 October. No payment is required to submit an idea. Selected teams must pay by 2 November via UPI or bank transfer.

  • At least one IEEE member: ₹500 per team
  • No IEEE members: ₹600 per team
  • Non-refundable after payment confirmation

Theme

Build around AI, IoT, sustainability, or smart healthcare.

Judging criteria

Innovation30%
Technical implementation25%
Impact25%
Presentation20%

Prize pool

₹50,000

For the best ideas and builds.

Schedule

Day 1: registration and development. Day 2: final presentations and awards.

Venue

To be announced. The hackathon will be hosted by AJIET, Mangaluru.

Submit your idea

Submit by 25 October 2026 at 11:59 PM. Upload your idea as a PowerPoint file (.ppt or .pptx, up to 5 MB). The top 20 teams will be announced on 30 October and notified. Only selected teams pay by 2 November via UPI or bank transfer: ₹500 per team with at least one IEEE member, or ₹600 per team with no IEEE members. Payment is non-refundable after confirmation.

Team and college

Member 1 is the team leader. Members 2 to 4 are optional, but if you start a member’s row, fill in all three fields.
